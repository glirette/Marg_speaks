### User-supplied clerk lead: separate 14-04420 case

Greg supplied an excerpt of a JeffNet case view labeled **State vs Terry Speaks,
14-04420**, Division J, showing an August 14, 2014 file date; a March 2015
registration-offense plea and eight-year commitment; a cross-reference to
pending **14-2321**; and a February 2022 entry labeled **Records Request Filed**.
The URL identified JeffNet's print case view, but this session reached only its
login screen. This is a **user-supplied transcript, not independently reviewed
clerk evidence**. The case number, offense and sentence differ from the
Lockhart murder appeal. A records-request entry does not by itself establish
who requested records, a post-conviction filing, any adjudication, a changed
sentence, a mandate, custody status, or release eligibility. Before citing it
in the case narrative, obtain the authenticated case view and match it to the
court's records; do not transfer its eight-year term to 14-2321 or to Sanchez.
