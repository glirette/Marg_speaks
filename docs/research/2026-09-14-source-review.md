## Findings affecting the new case file

- Keep a jury conviction and a guilty plea separate. Sanchez's plea was to
  manslaughter, not a jury conviction for the original murder charge.
- Preserve the difference between original sentencing and the later habitual-
  offender enhancement. A single life-sentence headline omits material context.
- Keep concurrent terms separate from an arithmetic total.
- Treat the 2017 Supreme Court step as attributed reporting until its underlying
  record has been obtained. Do not describe the report as a new trial, or use its
  date as a court-order date.
- Do not carry historical references to a facility or incarceration forward as a
  verified 2026 custody statement.

The current-source review confirms accessible historical sources. It does not
establish the absence of later proceedings.

## Search and access limitations

Focused public searches used combinations of Terry Speaks, Margaret Sanchez,
Jaren Lockhart, appeal, habeas and current years, including court-domain queries.
Some results concerned unrelated people with similar names, retrospective media,
or old articles carrying a current site footer. Those results were not adopted as
current legal-status evidence.

Attempts to open Louisiana Supreme Court opinion archive paths through the web
reader returned access/internal errors. No case-specific official action sheet was
retrieved. Search did not establish an exact Supreme Court docket/order identifier.
The site therefore preserves the WXXV attribution and the gap openly.

No authenticated clerk docket, PACER query, corrections locator, or direct court
records request was completed. No person or agency was contacted. Search is not
being represented as an exhaustive state/federal docket or custody check.
